# ReadyForPickup

ReadyForPickup is a macOS app that watches Apple Store pickup availability for iPhone and Mac and alerts you when your configuration is ready nearby.

## Download

**[Download ReadyForPickup](https://github.com/wehbs/ReadyForPickup/releases/latest/download/ReadyForPickup.dmg)** (the latest version, as a `.dmg`)

Earlier versions and release notes are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/wehbs/ReadyForPickup/releases).

## What it does

- Watches Apple Store pickup stock for current iPhones and Macs in 15 countries: the US, Australia, Austria, Canada, China mainland, France, Germany, India, Italy, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea, Spain, the United Arab Emirates and the UK
- Multiple saved searches, each with its own country, store and preferred models
- Alerts on this Mac, and optional Pushover phone notifications (bring your own Pushover app token)
- Auto-refresh with throttle backoff
